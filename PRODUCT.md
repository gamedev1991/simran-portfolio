A recruiter or hiring manager typically spends a short first visit (skimming, mobile or desktop) deciding whether to keep reading or move on, then may return to check specific case studies or credentials before a hiring decision or interview. The site is the primary artifact she links from a resume, LinkedIn, or application — it needs to stand alone without a live walkthrough.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single static HTML file, no backend, no build tooling.

- Must degrade gracefully for `prefers-reduced-motion` and keyboard/screen-reader access — this is a professional credibility site, not a demo, so accessibility failures directly undercut the positioning.

## Product Principles

1. Proof before flourish: every quantified claim and named client stays exact and verifiable; creative treatment wraps the evidence, never replaces it.
2. Words are the craft on display, not just the content — the site's own language and interaction design should demonstrate the same skill she's selling.
3. Read as senior and considered, not experimental for its own sake — this is a recruiting artifact, so "impressive" means polished and confident, not gimmicky or unfinished-feeling.
4. Signature moments should feel alive without demanding effort from the visitor — automatic over click-gated, but never at the cost of legibility or accessibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

`prefers-reduced-motion` must be honored throughout (no forced auto-animation for visitors who've opted out). Text must maintain readable contrast against every background it appears on — this was a specific, repeated point of prior feedback.
